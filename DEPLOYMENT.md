# 🚀 World-Class Deployment Guide (The 60-Second Way)

Since you are a **Full Stack & DevOps Engineer**, your deployment should be automated. Here is how to go live for **FREE**.

## 1. Upload to GitHub (DevOps First)
If you haven't already:
1. Initialize a Git repo in this folder.
2. Push all files to a **Public GitHub Repository**.
3. *The `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` I added will automatically show a "Passed" checkmark on your repo, proving your CI/CD knowledge.*

## 2. Deploy to Vercel (Award Winner Hosting)
1. Go to [Vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and log in with GitHub.
2. Click **"Add New"** -> **"Project"**.
3. Select your portfolio repository.
4. Click **"Deploy"**.
5. **BOOM!** Your 3D Universe is live at `yourname.vercel.app`.

## 3. The Final "Pro" Step
Once live, go to **Project Settings -> Domains** and add your own custom domain (if you buy one) to remove the `.vercel.app` suffix.

---

### **Why this way?**
*   **Global Speed:** Vercel uses an Edge CDN so your Three.js galaxies load in milliseconds.
*   **DevOps Identity:** Every time you push a change to GitHub, your site updates automatically.
*   **Zero Cost:** Free hosting forever.
